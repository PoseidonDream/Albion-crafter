How it's worked out

Recipes come from the game's own item files (ao-bin-dumps), Every tier and enchantment is included.

Return rate is 1 − 1 ÷ (1 + production bonus). Cities give 18% (refining in rest cities 15%), the city's specialty adds 15% for crafting or 40% for refining, focus adds 59%, and the daily bonus 10% or 20%. Artifacts, hearts, Avalonian energy, capes and other special resources are never returned.

Focus cost is the base cost × 0.5^(efficiency ÷ 10,000). Efficiency is 30 per mastery level, 250 per level of the item's own specialization, and 30 (18 for potions) per level of every specialization in the same mastery. Open an item to enter your levels, or type the focus cost you see in game to match it exactly.

Profit per craft = what you get after tax − resources you keep using after returns − station fee. Market tax is 4% with Premium or 8% without; placing a sell or buy order adds 2.5%. Silver per focus is the profit of one focused craft divided by its focus cost.

Prices come from the Albion Online Data Project, gathered by players running its client, for normal-quality items. Some prices are hours old and some items have none. Sold per day is the last 7 days ÷ 7. Check in game before a big buy.
